About Gracias Bakery

"Gracias" means "thank you". That is how we feel about every customer who walks in.

Our story

Gracias Bakery started with a simple idea: everyone in our neighborhood should be able to enjoy fresh, warm bread every morning.

We bake in small batches, starting before sunrise, so what you buy today was made today. From daily bread to birthday cakes, every item is made with care.

What we believe in

Fresh every day

We never sell yesterday's bread as today's. Everything is baked the same day.

Good ingredients

Quality flour, real butter and fresh eggs. No shortcuts.

Fair prices

Good bread should be affordable for every family.
